# Document engine build verification

Reviewed on November 22, 2026 by the development integrator. This records
the deterministic document-to-offer increment. Development-agent review is
not qualified independent human model validation or institutional approval.

## Delivered and observed

The evidence engine archives immutable document bytes and typed claims,
records explicit reviews, and projects current valid facts with provenance.
The probability engine calculates joint economic scenarios, terminal pursuit
value and separate risk policies using an exact CPU reference. The
application layer joins reviewed money fields to that engine atomically.
These paths use ordinary Python and do not require AI services.

The four-stage synthetic quote demonstration moves from unreviewed to
reviewed, then replacement pending to replacement reviewed. Offer B changes
from $47,000 to $27,000 baseline profit and from $36,700 to $16,700 expected
profit conditional on closing. The assumed conditional loss probability
changes from 5% to 30%; no revised offer passes the baseline profit rule.
The probabilities are teaching assumptions, not measured forecasts.

The build scheduler validates dependencies and phase gates, reserves one
integrator plus at most three workers, locks each named owner and intersecting
write scopes, and reports resource blocks and relative schedule estimates.
It does not launch agents or execute its proposed tickets.

Four reusable project agent profiles and a 14-skill/84-capability catalog
document the builder roles. Three implementation builders worked in parallel;
a separate fresh-context review tested representative skills against the
actual artifacts. Personal skill installation is separately verified and
does not create a runtime product agent.

## Reviewed findings and repairs

The evidence builder reproduced two material issues: illustrative evidence
could lose its label in a non-illustrative packet, and changed new-offer terms
could reuse the old probability context. Both were fixed and regression
tested. Changed live-counter and new-offer terms now hold the entire adjusted
result. Changed economics with externally estimated probabilities also hold
for a newly validated context. Synthetic provenance remains visible.

The fresh-context review challenged review authority, business-time versus
knowledge-time history, probability coverage and completion claims. Those
limits are explicit in the skills, guides and ticket evidence. The plan's
review history preserves these findings and their dispositions.

See the [ticket plan](document-engine-tickets.md),
[validation plan](../validation/document-engine-validation-plan.md), and
[initial segment hypothesis](initial-pilot-hypothesis.md). Completed ticket
statuses require a reviewed evidence record and complete prerequisite closure.

## Current limits

The quote documents and event probabilities are illustrative. R receives a
typed exchange, but R is unavailable in this environment. Real CUDA parity
and performance checks require hardware not available here. Reviewer names
are supplied by the calling application; production identity and review
independence need a service-level implementation. As-of projections use
declared business dates and do not establish complete knowledge-time replay.

Full financial coverage, generalized document reconciliation, empirical
probability calibration, multi-round negotiation, accessible review UI,
live connectors and scale economics remain separately tracked work.

No production service, runtime product agent, NVIDIA inference deployment,
external offer, signature, payment or real acquisition was performed.
