Run `python -m pilot.scenario` from the repository root and inspect `qualification.checks` followed by `underwriting.limits`. The Orchard fixture traces one district thesis to the parcel, legal use, coordinates, closed comparable records, costs and financing. Each evidence record includes its subject, reference, source, observation date and expiry. Three distinct comparable references must pass. Contrary current evidence holds the decision.

The monetary contract uses integer cents and basis points. The base calculation starts with resale proceeds after selling costs, then subtracts renovation, carrying costs and the base profit requirement. The downside calculation applies the stressed price and extra costs. Financing supplies the third purchase limit. The smallest ceiling binds. A blank financing cap, an unresolved title result, lack of road access or wetlands above 50% holds the bid for review. Positive surviving liens require a revised reviewed cost treatment before the pilot will qualify the purchase.

For the supplied case, the limits in dollars are 235,000 base, 242,400 downside and 254,000 financing.
